01 · Who I Am

About Me

Dedicated mechanical engineering professional with over 9 years of progressive experience in production planning & control, machine installation & commissioning, and preventive and breakdown maintenance within the tower manufacturing and tyre industries.

I currently lead production planning at Global Steel & Engineering Ltd. (GSEL), a steel tower manufacturer, where I build daily and weekly production schedules, prepare optimised cutting plans and material requirements for telecom and transmission tower projects, monitor machine downtime and output KPIs, and coordinate with procurement, quality control and logistics to keep production on schedule.

Earlier, at NDE Steel Structures, I led the installation and commissioning of CNC angle drilling and punching lines, power saws and overhead cranes. At Gazi Tyres, I supervised the mixing section and the commissioning of high-capacity Banbury mixing machines with automated silo feeding systems. Alongside plant work, I design and build practical software tools for the factory floor — including a deployed Cutting Optimization web application used for real production cutting plans.
